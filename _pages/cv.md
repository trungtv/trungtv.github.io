Education
======
* **PhD in Computer Science**, École normale supérieure Paris-Saclay (formerly ENS Cachan) / INRIA, France, 2013  
  Thesis: *Scalable data-management systems for Big Data*
* **M.S. in Computer Science**, ENS Cachan, France, 2009
* **B.S. in Computer Science**, Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), 2008

Appointments
======
* **Lecturer**, Department of Computer Science, School of Information and Communication Technology (SOICT), Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST)  
  Teaching databases, big data storage & processing, data science / visualization, and supervising student projects and theses

Industry & entrepreneurship
======
* **Co-founder**, [giasan.vn](https://gianhadat.cenhomes.vn) (2015–2018)  
  Real-estate analytics platform for Vietnam; 3rd prize, Talent of Vietnam Award 2017; exited in 2018
* Earlier roles as intern, engineer, and data scientist at **BKAV**, **Microsoft Research Cambridge**, **Trendiction** (Luxembourg), and **FPT Corporation**

Research interests
======
* Large-scale / distributed data management and analytics  
* Natural language processing and understanding (Vietnamese)  
* Applied machine learning for real-estate, healthcare, labor, and financial markets
