Viet-Trung Tran obtained his PhD in big data management and processing at ENS Cachan/INRIA France in 2013. He has particularly deep industrial experiences as being intern, full-time engineer, and adjoint data scientist at BKAV, Microsoft Research Cambridge, Trendiction Luxembourg, and FPT corporation. He is passionate in harnessing data for society. In 2015, he founded [giasan.vn](https://gianhadat.cenhomes.vn), the first real-estate analytic platform for Vietnam, that achieved the 3rd prize in Talent of Vietnam Award 2017, and successfully exited in 2018. His current research interests include large-scale data analytics for real-estate, healthcare, labor and financial markets.

He currently teaches and supervises student projects at the School of Information and Communication Technology (SOICT), Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).
